# The Korea Wave

**https://wave.koreasignals.com/** — *The business of Korean culture.* English-language industry analysis of Korean film, music, performance and entertainment.

We treat K-entertainment as an industry: weekly box-office and performing-arts digests computed from KOBIS (Korean Film Council) and KOPIS (Korea Performing Arts Box Office) data — revenue change, per-admission spend, and market concentration.

- [Editorial standards](https://wave.koreasignals.com/editorial-standards/)

Sister publication: [The Korea Pulse](https://pulse.koreasignals.com/) — Korea's economy and markets.
